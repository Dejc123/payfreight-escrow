PayFreight Escrow Explained

Bringing trust, speed, and digital euros to global logistics without the headache.

The Problem Today

Moving goods across borders is stressful. Shippers worry if they'll get paid, and carriers worry if their invoice will take 60 or 90 days to clear—or if they'll get paid at all. Traditional banking is slow, expensive, and loaded with middlemen.

Our Solution (The Escrow Concept)

We built a transparent, automated digital safe (an escrow) running on high-speed blockchain technology (Solana). Here is how it works in 3 simple steps:

  • Step 1 (Lock): The payer locks the funds (digital euros) into a secure, tamper-proof smart contract before the transport begins.
  • Step 2 (Transport): The truck drives, the cargo moves, and all logistics milestones are tracked digitally.
  • Step 3 (Release): Once delivery is verified, the smart contract automatically releases the money to the carrier instantly. No invoices lost in emails, no waiting 90 days.

Why This Matters for Adoption

Technology only wins if it solves real human problems. By removing friction, lowering fees, and guaranteeing payouts upon delivery, we make cross-border logistics safe for everyone—from small trucking companies to global enterprises.

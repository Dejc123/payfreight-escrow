# 🚀 PayFreight Protocol – Deployment & Setup Guide

This guide outlines the steps required to set up, build, test, and deploy the PayFreight smart contract and backend services.

---

## 1. Prerequisites
Ensure you have the following tools installed on your system:
* **Rust & Cargo** (Latest stable version)
* **Solana CLI** (`v1.16` or higher)
* **Anchor Framework** (`v0.29` or higher)
* **Node.js** (v18+) & **Yarn** / **npm**

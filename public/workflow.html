PayFreight Process Workflow

From paper CMR digitisation to automated escrow payout on Solana

1

1. Paper CMR to Digital (Off-Chain / Backend)

When the driver completes delivery, they capture a photo of the paper CMR. The backend service parses the image using Google Cloud Vision OCR, formats the data into structured JSON, and generates a unique SHA-256 cryptographic fingerprint.

Document Capture Google Cloud Vision OCR SHA-256 Hash
2

2. Smart Contract Verification (On-Chain / Solana)

The document hash is passed to the Solana smart contract. The contract verifies that the hash matches the initial booking record and confirms the consignee's digital e-POD signature before automatically releasing escrowed funds to the carrier.

Solana Smart Contract e-POD Signature Automated Escrow Release
3

3. Smart Contract Execution (Solana Anchor / Rust)

The entire verification and payout logic is executed via secure, high-throughput Rust smart contract instructions on Solana.

programs/payfreight/src/instructions/verify_and_release.rs

Technical Overview for Developers:

Verification and settlement are handled on-chain by the verify_and_release.rs instruction built with the Solana Anchor framework, guaranteeing near-instant finality, minimal transaction fees, and complete trustless automation.

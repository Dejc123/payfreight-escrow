How PayFreight Works

Bridging traditional paper logistics with Solana Web3 smart contracts: Transforming physical CMR documents into verified digital e-CMRs.

1

1. Paper CMR Digitisation (Off-Chain / Backend)

When goods are loaded or delivered, the driver captures a photo of the traditional paper CMR document using the mobile app. The PayFreight backend service (Node.js/Rust) processes the image using Google Cloud Vision OCR to extract text (consignor, recipient, weight, goods details) into a structured JSON dataset.

Document Scanning Google Cloud Vision OCR Structured JSON Output
2

2. Cryptographic e-CMR Creation (Digital Fingerprint)

To convert the digitized document into a tamper-proof e-CMR, the system generates a unique SHA-256 hash of the dataset. This cryptographic hash acts as the immutable digital fingerprint of the physical CMR document, securing its authenticity on-chain.

SHA-256 Hash Generation Digital Fingerprint e-CMR Identity
3

3. Smart Contract Verification & Escrow Release (On-Chain / Solana)

The generated cmr_hash is submitted to the Solana smart contract (via verify_and_release.rs). The contract checks if the hash matches the initial deal registration and confirms that an Electronic Proof of Delivery (e-POD) signature has been submitted. Once verified, escrowed funds are automatically released to the carrier.

Solana Smart Contract e-POD Verification Automated Payout

Technical Summary for Developers:

The Rust smart contract instruction verify_and_release.rs enforces two strict conditions on-chain:

1. require!(escrow.cmr_hash == submitted_cmr_hash) — Ensures the submitted document matches the original e-CMR.
2. require!(escrow.is_delivered == true) — Confirms that the consignee has verified the delivery (e-POD).

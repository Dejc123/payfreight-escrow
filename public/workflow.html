How Paper CMR Becomes Instant Payout

From a quick photo at the loading dock to verified proof of delivery and automated freight settlement.

1

1. Snap Paper CMR at Delivery

Upon unloading, the driver captures a photo of the signed paper CMR with a smartphone. The system automatically reads shipment details, stamps delivery time, and creates a clean digital Proof of Delivery (e-POD).

Mobile Photo Capture Zero Paperwork Delay Instant e-POD
2

2. Instant Document Verification

The system instantly validates shipment details against the original order. Shippers and dispatchers get immediate visibility, preventing document tampering, duplicate billing, or missing paperwork disputes.

Tamper-Proof Verification Real-Time Dispatch Alert Dispute Protection
3

3. Automated Carrier Payout

As soon as delivery proof is confirmed, escrowed freight funds are instantly released to the carrier. No more waiting 30 to 60 days for invoice maturity or mailing physical CMR copies across Europe.

Same-Day Payout Secured Escrow Funds Guaranteed Payment

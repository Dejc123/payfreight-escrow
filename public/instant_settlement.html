PayFreight.io

Instant Settlements Architecture (USA & Europe)
PayFreight delivers a streamlined global framework for modern freight settlements, bridging international supply chains with institutional-grade financial infrastructure powered by Solana and Circle.
Step 01 / Capital On-Ramp

Multi-Region Local Funding

Shippers settle freight charges locally using the financial channels they already trust. Fully regulated institutional reserves back the equivalent digital value in real time.

🇺🇸 USA Market (USD) Local funding via US ACH, Wire transfer, or corporate cards.
🇪🇺 Europe Market (EUR) Local funding via SEPA or SEPA Instant bank transfers.

The Storage: Regulated issuers like Circle hold the fiat reserves and instantly mint an exact 1:1 digital representation (USDC or EURC) on-chain.

Step 02 / On-Chain Escrow

Zero-Risk Global Vault

The newly minted stablecoins are instantly routed into an immutable smart contract (Solana Escrow Program).

This ensures complete financial transparency and security across international borders. The carrier has cryptographic verification that funds are secured and dedicated exclusively to the shipment.

Powered by Solana — Sub-second finality & fractions of a cent in fees
Step 03 / Milestone Execution

Automated Settlement Trigger

As cargo moves and digital proofs (such as e-CMR or verified IoT delivery data) are registered on-chain, the smart contract evaluates fulfillment parameters automatically.

Step 04 / Capital Off-Ramp

Direct Local Payouts

Upon successful delivery confirmation, the escrow vault releases funds, which seamlessly convert back to local fiat currency and deposit directly into the carrier's corporate bank account.

🇺🇸 USA Payout Converted via Circle API and routed directly to US business accounts via ACH / Wire.
🇪🇺 Europe Payout Converted via Circle API and deposited straight into corporate accounts via SEPA / IBAN.

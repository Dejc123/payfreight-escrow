Financial Architecture & e-CMR Escrow

Where does the money go and how are carrier funds secured?

For judges, investors, and carriers alike, the core question is: "Where is my money while the cargo is in transit?" PayFreight eliminates non-payment risks by utilizing a decentralized smart contract escrow vault acting as an unbreakable digital deposit guarantee.

1. Shipper Deposit

The transport commissioner (shipper) deposits funds into the smart contract vault prior to transit initiation. Funds are securely locked and isolated from liquidity risks.

2. e-CMR & Blockchain

As soon as the e-CMR (electronic consignment note) is digitally signed upon cargo delivery, the system automatically verifies successful fulfillment via cryptographic proofs.

3. Instant Payout

Funds are released from the vault directly to the carrier's account in a fraction of a second. No more 30 or 60-day payment delays, zero fraud exposure.

Carrier Fund Security Standard: Architecture built on immutable ledger technology, ensuring funds are safeguarded by military-grade cryptographic security standards. No entity – including platform administrators – can arbitrarily move funds outside the strict programmatic conditions defined in the e-CMR contract.

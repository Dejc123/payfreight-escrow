PayFreight Protocol

Global Standard for Freight Settlement | Powered by Solana (Zero-Volatility Stablecoin Rail)

Welcome to the official public architecture specification and execution roadmap for the PayFreight Protocol. We are building the infrastructure for a Global Standard for Freight Settlement—leveraging high-speed Solana Web3 architecture backed by stablecoins to completely eliminate crypto market volatility. Starting with rigorous testing and rollout across Europe and the United States, our vision is to establish a secure, instant, and borderless escrow standard worldwide.

Phase 1: Core MVP & Infrastructure Active 48h Sprint

  • Domain verification & enterprise email infrastructure via Resend (`payfreight.io`).
  • Backend mailer engine with automated transactional triggers (`escrow@`, `disputes@`).
  • 45-Minute TTL anti-exploit security warning integrated into the modal UX.
  • Live Solana mainnet transaction simulation & secure tokenized link verification.

Phase 2: Advanced Document Compliance & Multi-CMR Engine Next Up

  • Shipper Document Configuration: Shippers can define required document quantities during load creation (e.g., standard CMR/bill-of-lading counts, such as 1 to 6+ documents).
  • Neutral CMR Options: Optional toggle for specialized routing where secondary/neutral documents are generated to obscure origin/shipper identities from the end receiver.
  • Lieferschein & Pallet Slips: Optional checkboxes for mandatory delivery notes (Lieferschein) and pallet exchange certificates (EPAL or regional equivalents) required before payout release.
  • Carrier Multi-Upload Workflow: Dynamic interface with [+] Add Another CMR functionality allowing carriers to batch-upload all required compliance documents securely.

Phase 3: Secure Auth, 2FA & Global Institutional Dashboard Post-Launch

  • Frictionless gated user environment with Social & Email login options.
  • Google Authenticator (2FA): Mandatory or optional time-based one-time password (TOTP) verification for robust account security against hackers and financial fraud.
  • Comprehensive Shipper & Carrier Dashboards (My Transports, Freight in Progress, History).
  • In-app Mutual Cancellation & Dispute / Fault Attribution mediation tools.
  • Global multi-language and multi-currency localization expansion for major international trade routes.

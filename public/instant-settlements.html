PayFreight.io

Global Logistics Settlement Architecture (USA & Europe)
Forget sluggish banking networks, legacy intermediaries, and outdated payment terms. PayFreight introduces a unified global backbone for freight settlements—combining American market speed with European regulatory security, powered by Solana and Circle.
Step 01 / Capital On-Ramp

Multi-Region Local Funding

Shippers settle freight charges locally using the financial rails they already trust. In the background, fully regulated institutional reserves back the equivalent digital value instantly.

🇺🇸 USA Market (USD) Local funding via US ACH, Wire transfer, or corporate cards.
🇪🇺 Europe Market (EUR) Local funding via SEPA or SEPA Instant bank transfers.

The Storage: Regulated issuers like Circle hold the fiat reserves and instantly mint an exact 1:1 digital representation (USDC or EURC) on-chain.

Step 02 / On-Chain Escrow

Zero-Risk Global Vault

The newly minted stablecoins are instantly routed into a cryptographic, immutable smart contract (Solana Escrow Program).

This eliminates traditional factoring delays in the US and international cross-border friction in Europe. The carrier has cryptographic proof that funds are locked, verified, and untouchable by the shipper.

Powered by Solana (Sub-second finality & fractions of a cent in fees)
Step 03 / Milestone Execution

Automated Settlement Trigger

As cargo moves and digital proofs (such as e-CMR or verified IoT delivery data) are registered on the network, the smart contract evaluates fulfillment parameters instantly without manual intervention.

Step 04 / Capital Off-Ramp

Direct Local Payouts

Upon successful delivery confirmation, the escrow vault releases funds directly to the carrier's digital wallet, which seamlessly converts back to local fiat currency and deposits straight into their business bank account.

🇺🇸 USA Payout Converted via Circle API and routed directly to US business accounts via ACH / Wire.
🇪🇺 Europe Payout Converted via Circle API and deposited straight into local corporate accounts via SEPA / IBAN.

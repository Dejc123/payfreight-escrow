The Story of Carrier John: Why the Transport Industry Must Change

Dear partners and freight professionals,

Recently, we spoke with a carrier named John. For years, he worked tirelessly, driving day and night to ensure freight was always delivered on time. Yet, he faced one massive problem that plagues thousands in transport – waiting 60, 90, or even more days to receive hard-earned payments.

Despite having fully booked trucks, monthly expenses for fuel, road tolls, and driver salaries kept coming in relentlessly while payments for completed hauls lagged behind. When two major clients defaulted on their invoices, years of honest work crumbled, forcing John to close his business.

"This isn't just John's story – it's the daily reality for countless carriers across Europe. We believe this delayed payment system is completely unsustainable. Carriers deserve immediate, secure compensation upon completed delivery. That is why we are developing PayFreight.io."

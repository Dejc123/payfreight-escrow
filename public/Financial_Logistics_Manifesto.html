Hassle-Free Path to Payment in 4 Steps

Eliminating defaults, delays, and debt collection calls. No bank approvals or loans required.

1

Deal Agreement

Shipper and carrier agree on a load anywhere (on any exchange or directly) and enter the amount into the Payfreight interface.

2

SEPA Deposit (Lock)

Shipper transfers euros to a secure SEPA Escrow account. Carrier receives instant confirmation: "Funds locked, loading approved."

3

Delivery & POD

Carrier delivers the cargo and uploads the confirmed CMR / e-CMR or proof of delivery via smartphone or web.

4

Instant Bank Payout

The system automatically verifies the document and executes an Instant SEPA transfer directly to the carrier's bank account.

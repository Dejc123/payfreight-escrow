💡 How PayFreight Escrow Engine Works

Simple, Bank-grade & Instant Settlement Logic for Global Freight

⚡ Global Settlement Architecture (USD & EUR)
1

Shipper Locks Freight Payment

Shipper deposits payment via USD or EUR Bank Transfer (FedNow, ACH, Wire, SEPA Instant), Card or Crypto. Funds are locked safely in 1:1 USD or EUR value until cargo delivery.

2

Transport Execution & e-CMR / POD Verification

Transporter completes delivery and uploads e-CMR or Proof of Delivery (POD). System automatically verifies signatures and delivery milestones against protocol rules.

3

Instant Automated Bank Payout

Escrow automatically releases funds directly to carrier's bank account (via ACH/FedNow in US Dollars or SEPA Instant in Euros) or Settlement wallet. Zero delays, zero middleman fee gouging.

🔒 1. Non-Custodial Smart Lock ($ USD / € EUR)

Funds deposited in USD or EUR are held in automated smart vaults. PayFreight employees have zero manual access to move or withdraw locked funds unilaterally.

📄 2. e-CMR & POD Release Trigger

Release conditions are strictly programmatic. Payout in USD or EUR is triggered immediately upon cryptographic e-CMR verification or Proof of Delivery sign-off.

🏦 3. Direct Bank Payout (USD ACH/FedNow / EU SEPA)

Carriers do not need a crypto wallet. Payouts arrive directly to the carrier's US bank account via ACH/FedNow/Wire (USD) or European bank account via SEPA Instant (EUR).

Operates through licensed, US & EU regulated banking partners adhering strictly to ISO 20022 financial standards, US banking rules (ABA/ACH), and UN/CEFACT e-CMR protocols.

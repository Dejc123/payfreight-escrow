Zero hidden fees, zero volatility, and fully automated escrow protection.

1

Shipper Locks Freight Funds & Commitment

The shipper enters load details and securely locks the total freight amount + a €250 / $250 mutual commitment deposit in the escrow vault. The system instantly generates a secure, unlisted link for the carrier.

2

Carrier Review & Midnight Cancellation Window

The carrier opens the secure link, reviews the load, and stakes their €250 / $250 commitment deposit. Both parties retain the right to cancel strictly on the same calendar day until midnight (00:00 local time) with zero penalties—triggering an instant 100% refund of all funds and deposits back to the shipper!

3

Transport Execution & e-CMR Verification

Once the cancellation window passes and the load is active, the transport is executed. Upon delivery, the carrier uploads the signed e-CMR / Proof of Delivery (POD).

4

Instant Automated Bank Payout

The escrow engine automatically verifies delivery signatures and releases funds directly to the carrier's bank account (via ACH/FedNow for USD or SEPA Instant for EUR) within seconds. Clean, fast, and trustless!

💡 How PayFreight Escrow Engine Works

Simple, Bank-grade & Instant Settlement Logic for Logisticians

⚡ Money Flow Architecture (Simplified)
1

Shipper Locks Freight Payment

Shipper deposits payment via Bank Transfer, Card or Crypto into an automated escrow vault. Funds are locked safely in 1:1 EUR value until cargo delivery.

2

Transport Execution & e-CMR Verification

Transporter completes delivery and uploads e-CMR proof. System automatically verifies signatures and delivery milestones against protocol rules.

3

Instant Automated Payout

Escrow automatically releases funds directly to carrier's bank account (via SEPA Instant) or Settlement wallet. Zero delays, zero middleman fee gouging.

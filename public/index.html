💡 How PayFreight Escrow Engine Works

Simple, Bank-grade & Instant Settlement Logic for Global Freight

⚡ Global Settlement Architecture (USD & EUR)
1

Shipper Locks Freight Payment

Shipper deposits payment via USD or EUR Bank Transfer (FedNow, ACH, Wire, SEPA Instant), Card or Crypto. Funds are locked safely in 1:1 USD or EUR value until cargo delivery.

2

Transport Execution & e-CMR / POD Verification

Transporter completes delivery and uploads e-CMR or Proof of Delivery (POD). System automatically verifies signatures and delivery milestones against protocol rules.

3

Instant Automated Bank Payout

Escrow automatically releases funds directly to carrier's bank account (via ACH/FedNow in US Dollars or SEPA Instant in Euros) or Settlement wallet. Zero delays, zero middleman fee gouging.
